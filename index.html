About the Shop

A Family Shop. A Craftsman's Standard.

For more than 25 years, Downtown Auto Detailing & Repair has been San Bruno's trusted destination for meticulous detailing and expert automotive service. Jaime and his crew treat every vehicle like their own — from daily drivers to concours-ready classics.

We specialize in Ford, Chevy, Tesla, Lexus, Subaru, Mercedes, GMC, Porsche, Cadillac, Volvo and more. Every job is finished to exceed expectations — because we're passionate about cars, and we do it right.
